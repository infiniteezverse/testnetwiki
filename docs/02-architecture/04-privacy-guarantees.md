# Privacy Guarantees

The cryptographic architecture provides comprehensive protection across all operational fronts:

* **Unlinkable Transfers:** Deposit transactions and eventual withdrawal transactions cannot be linked by public observers or chain analytics tools.
* **Asset & Balance Masking:** Held assets and token amounts are fully obscured inside Poseidon commitments.
* **Hidden Order Intent:** Limit order prices and quantities remain completely invisible until matched by the operator.
* **Shielded LP Positions:** Liquidity providers hold pool shares as cryptographic commitments, obscuring capital concentration levels from external analysis.
