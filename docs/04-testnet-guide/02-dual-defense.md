# Dual Defense Program & Transition Strategy

The testnet launch runs in parallel with the **Hacken Dual Defense White Hat Reward Program**:

* **Active Audit Testing:** Security researchers and white-hat developers are encouraged to inspect deployed smart contracts, test verifier circuits, and submit vulnerability reports through the official program.
* **Continuation Beyond Dual Defense:** The `testnet.zendex.fi` platform will remain operational past the conclusion of the Hacken Dual Defense campaign. Testnet operations will continue until all security recommendations are integrated, circuits are optimized, and community consensus grants final deployment approval for Horizen Mainnet.
