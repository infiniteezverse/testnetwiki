# Vision for Advanced EVM Environments

Financial privacy is a prerequisite for fair and efficient market structures. ZENDEX serves as the foundational private execution primitives layer for the Horizen ecosystem. By providing private swapping, hidden limit order matching, and zero-knowledge identity attestations, ZENDEX enables high-frequency market makers, treasury managers, and retail traders to interact safely within an EVM-compatible environment without compromising strategy or operational privacy.
