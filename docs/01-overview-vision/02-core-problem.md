# The Core Problem

Public blockchains expose:

* **Mempool Visibility & MEV Extraction:** Public transaction pools allow searchers to front-run, back-run, and sandwich trades, resulting in significant value extraction from traders.
* **Strategy & Balance Leakage:** Wallet balances, trade volumes, historical activity, and active limit orders are permanently viewable on-chain, exposing quantitative strategies and capital movements to competitors.
* **Institutional Capital Hesitancy:** Institutional asset managers and liquidity providers are legally or competitively restricted from deploying capital into environments where positions, trade size, and counterparties are visible to the public.
